Quiz fact: in an experiment where horses wore cloth coats of different colours, horses in striped coats had far fewer horse flies touching and landing on them than when wearing black or white coats; close up, the stripes stopped flies from making a controlled landing (Caro et al., "Benefits of zebra stripes: Behaviour of tabanid flies around zebras and horses", PLoS One, 2019).
