# The bridge designed without the wind

**File:** `019-tay-bridge.mp4` · 1080x1920 · 48.6 s

## Description

On December 28, 1879, during a violent storm, the high girders of the first Tay Rail Bridge fell into the Firth of Tay as a passenger train crossed. There were no survivors; as many as 75 people died. The design made no explicit allowance for wind loading.

## Script

What happens when you design a bridge, and leave out the wind? Scotland found out, in 1879.

The Tay Bridge opened in 1878. In the middle, trains ran inside tall iron girders, high above the water.

Its designer made no explicit allowance for wind. And the iron castings that held the bracing were flawed.

December 28th. A violent storm hits the bridge side-on. A passenger train starts across.

At 7:16 that evening, the high girders fell into the river. The train went with them.

There were no survivors. As many as 75 people died.
