## Script

A wave of molasses, 25 feet high, moving at 35 miles an hour. This really happened, in Boston, in 1919.

The tank stood 50 feet tall and 90 feet across, and held up to 2.3 million gallons.

It leaked so badly that it was painted brown to hide the leaks.

A 2014 engineering analysis found the steel was half as thick as it should have been, even by the standards of the time.

On January 15th, after a rapid rise in temperature, the tank burst.

21 people were killed, and 150 were injured.

After three years of hearings, the owner was found responsible, and paid $628,000 in damages.
