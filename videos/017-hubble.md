# Hubble launched with a mirror ground to the wrong shape

## Description

The Hubble Space Telescope was launched on April 24, 1990 with a 2.4 m mirror whose outer edge was too flat by about 2,200 nanometres. A testing device had been assembled with one lens 1.3 mm out of position. Corrective optics were installed in December 1993.

## Script

The Hubble Space Telescope reached orbit in 1990. Then its first images came back blurred.

Its main mirror is 2.4 meters wide. And its outer edge had been polished too flat, by about 2,200 nanometers.

That is about one four hundred and fiftieth of a millimeter. Enough to stop the light from meeting at a single point.

The cause was a testing device used to check the mirror's shape. One lens inside it sat 1.3 millimeters out of position.

Other tests did report the error. They were dismissed, because the faulty device was considered more accurate.

In December 1993, astronauts flew up on the shuttle Endeavour and installed corrective optics. The telescope could finally see clearly.
