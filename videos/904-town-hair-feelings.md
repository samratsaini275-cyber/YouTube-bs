## Script

In this town, hair shows feelings.

Red means angry.

Blue means sad.

Pink means you're in love.

And yellow means you're nervous.

This is Dave. His hair is always yellow.

He is late for everything.

Missus Lee's hair goes red when someone parks outside her house.

Barry sells hats. Five dollars each. Business is great.

And the mayor? He shaved his head.

Nobody knows how he feels. Ever.

And me? I'm Milo.

Rosie's hair has been pink since our bowling date.

Tonight, I was going to tell her I love her.

My hair went yellow. Then very yellow.

So I bought one of Barry's hats.

Sunset. A picnic in the park.

Then the wind blew my hat off.

My hair was glowing like a lamp.

Rosie laughed.

I said: Rosie, I love you.

Her hair went pink. Then very, very pink.

Then it went every colour at once.

Nobody in town had ever seen that before.

Then a big black hat blew past.

It was the mayor's. And his hair had grown back.

Bright pink.

He was holding hands with Missus Lee.

The next morning, Barry sold every hat in town.
