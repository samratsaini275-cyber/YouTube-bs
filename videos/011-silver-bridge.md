## Script

A crack one tenth of an inch deep. That is all it took to bring down this entire bridge.

The Silver Bridge was built in 1928, and carried traffic over the Ohio River at Point Pleasant, West Virginia.

It hung from chains of steel eyebars, not wire cables. Each link in the chain was just two eyebars, side by side.

That left little to no redundancy. At a bearing inside one eyebar, number 330, a small crack formed, and slowly grew through corrosion.

On December 15th, 1967, in rush hour traffic, that eyebar broke. The bridge collapsed, and 46 people died.

Investigators found no sign the bridge had been overloaded. The collapse led to the first national bridge inspection program in the United States, in 1968.
