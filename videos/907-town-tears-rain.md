In this town, tears make rain.

Cry, and a little cloud appears over your head.

Even happy tears.

Dave cried at a sad movie. The street flooded.

Missus Lee never cries. Her garden is very dry.

Barry has an umbrella stand. Five dollars each.

The mayor cried once. Now there's a lake.

And me? I'm Milo.

Today, I married Rosie.

Everyone promised not to cry.

Then Rosie walked down the aisle.

Her dad cried first.

Then Missus Lee. For the first time ever.

Then the mayor. Then Dave. Then everyone.

Soon the water was up to our knees.

Then up to our waists.

Barry did the ceremony. Five dollars.

He said: you may now kiss the bride.

So we kissed.

Everyone cheered. And the rain stopped.

So the whole town went for a swim. In their best clothes.

And Mister Biscuit said what everyone was thinking.

Best. Wedding. Ever.
