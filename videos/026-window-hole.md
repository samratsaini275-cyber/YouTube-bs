## Script

There is a hole in your airplane window. Miles above the ground. And someone put it there on purpose.

Your window is not one sheet of glass. It is three.

The tiny hole is in the middle one.

Up there, the cabin air is kept at a much higher pressure than the thin air outside. And all of it pushes on your window.

The hole lets cabin air into the gap. So the outer pane takes the load. If a pane ever gives way, it is that one. The middle pane is still there.

Bonus: it lets moisture out of the gap, so your view stays mostly free of fog.
