# The warning telegram that came too late

**File:** `015-quebec-bridge.mp4` · 1080x1920 · 60.2 s

## Description

On August 29, 1907, the south arm of the unfinished Quebec Bridge collapsed into the St. Lawrence River in 15 seconds, killing 75 of the 86 workers on it. A telegram ordering no more load was not passed on in time.

## Script

A telegram was sent to stop work on this bridge. It was not passed on in time. That afternoon, 75 men died.

Quebec, 1907. A giant steel bridge is growing out over the St. Lawrence River.

The early calculations were never properly checked. The bridge's own weight was far more than it could carry.

That summer, the site engineer saw steel members bending. At first, he was told the problem was minor.

On August 29th, the consulting engineer telegraphed: add no more load. The message was not passed on to Quebec in time.

Near quitting time, the south arm fell into the river in 15 seconds. Of 86 workers on the bridge, 75 were killed.
