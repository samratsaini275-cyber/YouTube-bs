# This bridge was shut two days after opening — the crowd made it wobble

## Description

London's Millennium Bridge opened on June 10, 2000 and was closed on June 12 after unexpected sideways vibration. The cause was synchronous lateral excitation: pedestrians unconsciously matching their steps to the sway.

## Script

This bridge was shut two days after it opened. The problem was the people walking on it.

June 10th, 2000. 90,000 people cross on opening day, with up to 2,000 on the bridge at a time.

The deck begins to sway sideways. Without realizing it, people start to match their footsteps to the sway.

Their steps push the bridge further, which pulls more people into step. This feedback loop is called synchronous lateral excitation.

The bridge was closed on June 12th, and stayed closed for almost two years.

The fix: 37 fluid dampers and 52 tuned mass dampers, at a cost of £5 million.

It reopened in February 2002. Londoners nicknamed it the Wobbly Bridge.
