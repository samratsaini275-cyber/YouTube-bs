## Script

It is late. Your eyes close for a second. And the car starts to drift.

Then: this. The whole car shakes, and a loud rumble fills the cabin.

Those grooves are rumble strips. They are cut into the road on purpose, to jolt drowsy and distracted drivers.

You find them along the edge of the road, and down the center line. Right where a drifting car would cross.

In one Swedish study, sleep-deprived drivers in a simulator perked up after hitting the strip.

Across many studies, crashes dropped by 27 percent on average. And cars running off the road? Down by up to 80 percent in some.
