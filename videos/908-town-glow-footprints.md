In this town, footprints glow.

Every step you take glows for a whole day.

Dave's footprints go in circles. He gets lost a lot.

Missus Lee's go to the mayor's house. Every night.

Barry has a shoe cover stand. Five dollars a pair.

And me? I'm Milo. I just got married.

This morning, our wedding cake was gone.

Someone had eaten the whole thing.

So we followed the footprints.

Out the kitchen door. Past the bakery. Past the lake.

Into the town hall. Up the stairs.

Small footprints. Very small. With four feet.

They stopped at a door.

Inside was Mister Biscuit.

Sitting on the desk. Very full. Covered in icing.

Rosie asked: did you eat our cake?

He said what he was thinking.

Yes. Then he burped.

His only words: best cake ever.
