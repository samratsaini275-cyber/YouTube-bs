# The store that stayed open while it cracked

**File:** `016-sampoong.mp4` · 1080x1920 · 59.7 s

## Description

The Sampoong Department Store in Seoul collapsed at 5:52 p.m. on June 29, 1995, killing 502 people. Columns were thinner than the plans required, a fifth floor had been added, and cracks seen that day did not lead to an evacuation.

## Script

Cracks were spreading across the top floor of this store. Management kept it open. That evening, it collapsed in under 20 seconds.

Seoul, 1995. The Sampoong Department Store was first planned as a four floor apartment building.

Then a fifth floor was added.

And the columns were built 60 centimeters wide, not the 80 the plans required.

On the roof sat three air conditioning units, 15 tonnes each. In 1993, they were dragged across it, cracking the slab.

On June 29th, the cracks multiplied. Directors urged an evacuation. The chairman refused.

At 5:52 that evening, the floor around one column gave way. 502 people died.
