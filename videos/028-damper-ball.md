## Script

High inside this skyscraper, a steel ball weighing 660 tonnes hangs on cables. Above the people below.

This is Taipei 101. Strong gusts push on the tower, and make it sway.

So engineers hung a giant pendulum. From the 92nd floor, down to the 88th.

When the tower leans one way, the ball swings the other way and soaks up the motion. It can cut the sway by up to 40 percent.

The ball is 5.5 meters across. Made of 41 steel plates, welded together.

In 2015, Typhoon Soudelor hit. The ball swung a full meter. The biggest move it has ever made.
