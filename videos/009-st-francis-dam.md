## Script

The chief engineer inspected this dam, and judged it safe. That same night, it collapsed.

The St. Francis Dam stood in a canyon north of Los Angeles, 185 feet above the stream bed.

While it was being planned and built, its height was raised by ten feet, to store more water.

On March 12th, 1928, William Mulholland inspected a new leak. He concluded it was not dangerous.

Two and a half minutes before midnight, the dam failed. 12.4 billion gallons surged down the canyon, in a wave up to 140 feet high.

The flood ran 54 miles to the Pacific Ocean, reaching it around 5:30 in the morning. At least 431 people died.

The dam failed because of defective foundations. Mulholland told the inquest: If there was an error in human judgment, I was the human.
