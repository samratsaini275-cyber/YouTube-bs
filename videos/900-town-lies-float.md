In this town, lies float.

Every lie you tell becomes a balloon.

Tied to your wrist. All day.

Small lies, small balloons.

Big lies? Huge balloons.

This is Dave.

The biggest balloon in town history.

Three balloons. One table.

The whole dinner floated out the window.

We passed the mayor. He waved.

We passed Dave. He said he'd be down in five minutes.

Then Barry flew by. Five dollars each, he said.

Rosie looked at me and said: best date ever.
