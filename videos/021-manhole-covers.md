# Why manhole covers are round

**File:** `021-manhole-covers.mp4` · 1080x1920 · 51.8 s

## Description

A round manhole cover cannot fall through its own opening; a square one can, if it is turned diagonally. Round covers also need no lining up, can be rolled, and sit on a round shaft, the strongest shape against the earth around it.

## Script

Why are manhole covers round? It is not for looks. A round cover can never fall down its own hole.

Try it with a square one. Turn it on its edge, line it up with the diagonal, and it drops straight through.

A circle is the same width in every direction. So however you turn it, it cannot fit through.

There is more. A round cover never needs lining up. Drop it in at any angle, and it fits.

And these things are heavy. A cast iron cover can weigh up to 300 pounds. A round one can be rolled.

One more reason. The shaft below is a round tube: the strongest shape against the earth pressing in around it.
