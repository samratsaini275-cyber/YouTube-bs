## Script

This was one of the most powerfully armed warships in the world. It sank 1,300 meters into its first voyage.

August 10th, 1628. The Vasa leaves Stockholm, with 64 guns on two full gun decks.

But she was top-heavy, with too much weight in the upper part of the hull.

Before she sailed, 30 men ran back and forth across the deck to test her. After only three trips, the admiral stopped the test, fearing she would capsize.

She was ordered to sea anyway. A gust heeled her over, the open lower gunports went under, and water rushed in.

About 30 people died. An inquiry was held, and no one was punished.

Four of the builders' rulers have been found. Two measure in Swedish feet, two in Amsterdam feet. The ship was raised in 1961, with her hull largely intact.
