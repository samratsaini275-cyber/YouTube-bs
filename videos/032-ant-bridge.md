## Script

This is a suspension bridge. It was built for exactly one ant. His name is Gary.

Gary needed to cross a puddle. The engineers looked at the puddle. And they overthought it.

Two towers. Steel cables. A toll booth. Gary does not have money.

Opening day. One ribbon. A crowd of zero.

Gary walks out to the middle. Looks down. And walks back.

Real fact: army ants build bridges out of their own bodies. Gary could have just called his friends.
